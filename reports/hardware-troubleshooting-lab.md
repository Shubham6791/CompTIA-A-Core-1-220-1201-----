# Hardware Troubleshooting Lab

## Objective
To document practical learning about computer hardware, common hardware faults, and systematic troubleshooting as part of my CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201) preparation.

## Areas of Study
- CPU, RAM, motherboard, and storage
- HDD vs SSD
- Power supply and cooling
- BIOS/UEFI fundamentals
- Common hardware symptoms and troubleshooting

## Troubleshooting Method
1. Identify the reported symptom.
2. Check power, cables, and physical connections.
3. Gather information and inspect error messages.
4. Test one possible cause at a time.
5. Verify whether the issue is resolved.
6. Record the findings and preventive steps.
